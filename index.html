03 / Your action report

Awaiting scan

Your report will show tracking coverage, an estimated arm-up phase, follow-through review frames and observed screen-plane movement. It will also explain what could not be assessed.

This is 2D movement analysis, not a validated bowling coach, a legality test, or a medical assessment. An arm-up frame is not a confirmed ball-release frame.
